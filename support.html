MARATHONGO

Support

MarathonGo helps runners find their next marathon and plan everything around it: race dates, ballot and entry reminders, course facts, trip costs and a log of the marathons you've run.

Contact

Email tyiannis@yahoo.com. We usually reply within a few days. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working.

Spotted wrong race information?

Race dates, entry status and fees come from official race websites and are checked regularly, but organisers change things. Email us the race name and a link to the official page and we'll correct it. Always confirm details on the race's official website before booking.

Common questions

Reminders don't arrive. Check Settings → Notifications → MarathonGo and make sure notifications are allowed. Reminders are set for the races you've saved and the "Remind me on" dates you've chosen.

How do I connect Apple Health? Open Me → My next race → Connect Apple Health and allow access to workouts. To change it later: Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → MarathonGo.

How do I add the widget? Touch and hold an empty area of your home screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, search for MarathonGo and choose Race countdown.

Why does a race say "expected date"? Dates after the next confirmed race are worked out from the race's usual weekend. They change to confirmed once the organiser announces them.

What does "Open for entry" show? Races where we've confirmed that entry or a ballot is open right now. Races we haven't been able to check yet are counted separately.

Where is my data stored? Only on your iPhone. There's no account, and nothing is uploaded. See the privacy policy.

How do I delete my data? Delete items in the app, or delete the app to remove everything.
